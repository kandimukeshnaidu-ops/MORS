# Welcome to MORS (Closed Beta Tester Guide)

**Welcome to the MORS Closed Beta!**

MORS is a social food journal — a personal, searchable archive of your whole food life, plus a quiet community built around taste. It is **not** a delivery app, booking engine, or noisy review site.

---

## 1. Quick Start in 3 Steps

1. **Sign Up:**
   - Head to `http://localhost:3000/auth/register` (or the beta staging URL).
   - Enter your Beta Invite Code: `HYDERABAD-BETA-2026`
   - Complete your profile with your preferred username.

2. **Log Your First Experience:**
   - Tap the **Log** button (or `+` on mobile).
   - Choose a photo or snap one directly.
   - Enter your dish (e.g., *Hyderabadi Mutton Dum Biryani* or *Homemade Dal Tadka*).
   - Rate it with half stars (0.5 to 5.0) and publish!

3. **Check Your Personal Diary:**
   - Click **Diary** in the navigation bar to see your chronological taste archive.
   - Switch between **Timeline** and **Calendar** view anytime.

---

## 2. Important Beta Etiquette & Privacy

- **Home-cooked food is first-class:** You don't need a restaurant, a bill, or a location to log your food.
- **Your Privacy Matters:** Setting an entry to **Private** or your profile to **Private** guarantees that nobody without approved follower status can see your memories.
- **Photos:** EXIF and GPS location data are automatically stripped on your device before upload to safeguard your home location.

---

## 3. Reporting Feedback & Bugs

Found something that felt confusing, slow, or broken?
- Drop a note via the in-app feedback modal or email `beta@mors.food`.
- State what you tried to do, what happened, and your device / browser model.
