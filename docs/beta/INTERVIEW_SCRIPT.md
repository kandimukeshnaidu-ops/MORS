# MORS Closed Beta — User Interview Script

**Format:** 30-minute 1-on-1 moderated session (video call / screen-share)  
**Tone:** Open, inquisitive, non-defensive. Listen 80%, speak 20%.

---

## 1. Warm-Up & Context (5 mins)
- "Tell me a bit about how you usually remember the meals and places you love right now? (e.g. Camera roll, saved Instagram posts, Notes app, Google Maps lists?)"
- "How frequently do you find yourself wanting to look up something you ate weeks or months ago?"

---

## 2. Walkthrough & Think-Aloud (15 mins)
Ask the participant to share their screen and complete two live tasks:
1. **Task 1: The Quick Log**
   - "Imagine you just finished eating dinner at home or at a cafe. Could you log that experience into MORS right now?"
   - *Observe:* Where do they hesitate? Do they search for a dish or type a custom one? Is rating intuitive? Does the upload finish quickly?
2. **Task 2: Searching Your History**
   - "Show me how you would find a specific dish or meal you logged a few days ago in your Diary."
   - *Observe:* Do they use search, calendar, or category filters?

---

## 3. Deep Dive Questions (8 mins)
- "What did you try to do in MORS that you couldn't do?"
- "Did you feel completely in control of who sees what you log?"
- "How did the visual feel of the app feel compared to typical delivery or restaurant apps?"
- "What was the most delightful moment you encountered?"

---

## 4. Wrap-Up & Next Steps (2 mins)
- Thank them for their candid feedback and invite them to continue logging through Week 2 and 3.
