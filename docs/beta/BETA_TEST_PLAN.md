# MORS Closed Beta Test Plan (Hyderabad Cohort)

**File:** `docs/beta/BETA_TEST_PLAN.md`  
**Milestone:** M3 Closed Beta — Work Package WP-3.2  
**Cohort Target:** 10–20 food lovers, home cooks, and restaurant explorers in Hyderabad  
**Duration:** 3 Weeks + Analysis  
**Access Model:** Closed Beta Invite Codes (`HYDERABAD-BETA-2026`, `MORS-FOUNDER-BETA`, `TASTE-ARCHIVE-HYD`)

---

## 1. Objectives & Success Metrics
1. **Effortless Logging:** Validate that users can complete a Quick Log in **under 60 seconds** with ≤ 3 interactions.
2. **Personal Archive:** Verify repeat diary revisits and filtering across dishes, dates, and categories.
3. **Genuine Social Discovery:** Verify chronological feed consumption, mutual follow, and respectful taste sharing.
4. **Privacy Invariance:** Verify that private and followers-only entries never leak to unpermitted accounts.

### Quantitative Targets (Directional)
- ≥ 70% signup-to-onboarding completion
- ≥ 80% of testers publish at least one food experience within 24 hours of joining
- ≥ 35% of testers log meals on 3+ distinct days
- Median Quick-Log duration < 45 seconds

---

## 2. Tester Persona Allocation

| Persona Group | Target Count | Key Profiles in Hyderabad | Primary Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Street & Heritage Foodies** | 4–5 | Old City, Charminar, RTC X Roads | Irani chai, biryani, street dosa logs |
| **Passionate Home Cooks** | 4–5 | Home culinary experimenters | Home-cooked meals without places or prices |
| **Fine Dining / Cafe Explorers**| 3–4 | Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills | Multi-dish tasting menus, aesthetic food photos |
| **Non-Technical Testers** | 2–3 | Casual diners | Verifying clarity of language and zero jargon |

---

## 3. Mandatory Tester Journey Checklist

Every participant is requested to complete the following 8 actions during Week 1:
1. **Join & Onboard:** Sign up using their invite code, set up display name and 18+ age gate.
2. **Log a Home-Cooked Meal:** Publish an entry with a photo and zero place attached.
3. **Log a Multi-Dish Restaurant Meal:** Select a Hyderabad restaurant (e.g., Paradise, Niloufer) and add 2+ dishes with half-star ratings (0.5..5.0).
4. **Explore the Diary:** Browse past eating occasions on the timeline and filter by category and rating.
5. **Follow Peers:** Find and follow fellow testers; view their chronological meal feed.
6. **Curate a Collection:** Create a public or unlisted collection (e.g. "Best Biryanis in Hyderabad") and add dishes and places.
7. **Social Interaction:** Like a fellow food lover's log and leave an encouraging culinary comment.
8. **Privacy Test:** Create a private journal entry and verify that fellow testers cannot view it.
